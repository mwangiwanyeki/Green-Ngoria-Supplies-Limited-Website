## Background

Formed to provide Gold and Gemstone Mining, Civil Works, Building Works, Timber Importation, Oil
Importation and Supplies. Technical departments headed by qualified engineers; qualified staff for
project construction, supervision and supplies. Started in Tanzania pursuing mining activities,
later extended to Bondo (Siaya County, Kenya) and Taita Taveta.

Six growth strategies (from the Mining section): **Growing production, Growing margins, Growing
partnerships, Growing people, Growing reserves, Growing safety.**

## Service Divisions

1. **Gold Mining** — all aspects of gold mining services. Sites: Bondo (Siaya County, Kenya),
   Taita Taveta, Tanzania. Gold processing plant at Bondo, Siaya.
2. **Gemstone Mining** — international gemstone mining, development and marketing. Owns two
   gemstone mines (one Kenya, one Tanzania).
3. **Building Works** — new buildings, renovation, restoration, partitioning, finishes, painting.
4. **Road Construction** — site clearance, sub-base/base formation, soil stabilization, asphaltic
   concrete laying, surface dressing, AC overlays, bridges, box culverts, storm drainage systems.
5. **Water Projects** — water supply projects, water reticulation systems, water and sewerage.
6. **Mechanical** — 10+ years of mechanical works contracts.
7. **Electrical Services** — design & construct, commissioning, maintenance support, electrical /
   instrumentation / communication services, product installation, testing & commissioning,
   preventive maintenance, automation, energy-saving solutions. Sectors served: industry,
   financial services, insurance, IT, manufacturing, government ministries.
8. **Oil Importation** — six divisions: Petroleum, Lubricants, Chemicals, Logistics,
   Infrastructure, Retail.
   - *Petroleum products:* Diesel, Petrol, Paraffin, Heavy Fuel Oil (HFO), LPG, Gases & Gas
     Mixtures, Jet Fuel, Aviation Gasoline, Bitumen.
   - *Lubricants:* Automotive, On-Road Transportation (trucks & buses), Off-Road Equipment
     (earth-moving machinery), Marine & Railroad, Industrial.
   - *Chemicals:* Polymers, Solvents, Fertilisers.
   - *Infrastructure:* site selection, depot design, regulatory compliance & approval, construction.
   - *Retail:* design and development of fuel stations for new and existing businesses.
   - Reach: fuel optimisation solutions for clients in all 47 counties of Kenya.
9. **Timber Importation** — Certified Deciduous Hardwood Sawn Timber; Certified Hardwood &
   Softwood Round Logs; Certified Tropical Hardwood; Coniferous Softwood; Crane Mats, Skid Mats &
   Fenders; Custom Milled Special Sizes; Decking / Burmese Teak Decking; Dunnage & Shoring Timber;
   Hardwood Railroad Ties / Marine Timber Piling; Rustic Furniture & Live Edge Tabletop Slabs;
   Timber Seasoning & Treatment; Utility Poles, Wooden Bollards, Fence Posts.
   - *Product categories:* Tropical Hardwood Timber standard & special sizes (D70 & D60 grade),
     Softwood standard & special sizes, Pressure Treated Timber, Bespoke Timber Products, Heavy
     Duty Timber Decking, Timber Posts & Bollards, Plywood.
   - *Applications:* External applications, heavy construction, fabrication yards, marine
     (shipyards, dry docks), civil construction works, oil field development.
10. **General Supplies** — general merchandise, office supplies, relief supplies. Clients in
    consumer companies, financial sector, NGO sector, government sector.

## Health, Safety & Environment

Comprehensive Health and Safety policy covering employees and the public. Priority on Environment,
Health and Safety (EHS) in all projects; EHS treated as a competitive strength benefiting clients,
shareholders and employees. Management commits to continual EHS improvement via objectives and
targets, training, new ideas, employee participation, coordination with clients and suppliers, and
annual management reviews.
